1 · Warm-up

Squaring a bracket gives three terms, not two

Multiply out(x + 3)2
  1. (x + 3)(x + 3)Squared means times itself.
  2. x2 + 3x + 3x + 9Each term in the first bracket times each term in the second.
  3. x2 + 6x + 9Middle: 3x + 3x = 6x. End: 3 × 3 = 9.
The pattern (x + a)2 = x2 + 2ax + a2 (x − a)2 = x2 − 2ax + a2

An expression like x2 + 6x + 9, which equals a bracket squared, is called a perfect square.

The middle number is the number in front of x. In x2 + 6x + 9 it's 6, which is twice the 3 in the bracket. So:

  • the number in the bracket is half the middle number
  • the last number is that half, squared

That is the whole idea behind completing the square.

2 · The move

Half it, square it

Now go backwards. Start with x2 + 6x. What number do you add to make a perfect square?

Half of 6 is 3, so the bracket is (x + 3). The number to add is 32 = 9.

  1. Half it. Take half of the middle number. Keep its sign.
  2. Square it. That's the number to add. It is never negative.
  3. Write the bracket. The bracket holds x and the half: (x + half)2.
Make a perfect squarex2 + 6x + ___ = ( ___ )2
  1. half of 6 is 3Half it.
  2. 32 = 9Square it. 9 goes in the blank.
  3. x2 + 6x + 9 = (x + 3)2The bracket holds the half. It's section 1's example, backwards.
Make a perfect squarex2 − 8x + ___ = ( ___ )2
  1. half of −8 is −4Half of a negative number is negative.
  2. (−4)2 = 16Negative times negative is positive: (−4)(−4) = 16.
  3. x2 − 8x + 16 = (x − 4)2The bracket holds the half, minus sign and all. Check the middle term: 2 · (−4) · x = −8x.
Watch the signs
  • The half has the same sign as the middle number.
  • The number you add is always positive.
  • The sign in the bracket matches the middle number: x2 − 8x gives (x − 4)2.
When the middle number is odd

Same steps, with fractions. Half of 5 is 52, and its square is 254, so x2 + 5x + 254 = (x + 52)2. Keep fractions, not decimals.

4 · Keep it balanced

In an equation, add it to both sides

An equation is a balance. Whatever you add to the left side, add to the right side too.

Solve by completing the squarex2 − 6x = 7
  1. half of −6 is −3, and (−3)2 = 9This is the number to add.
  2. x2 − 6x + 9 = 7 + 9Add 9 to both sides.
  3. (x − 3)2 = 16The left side is now a perfect square.
  4. x − 3 = ±4Take the square root of both sides. Don't forget the ±.
  5. x = 7  or  x = −1Add 3. Check: 49 − 42 = 7 and 1 + 6 = 7.
