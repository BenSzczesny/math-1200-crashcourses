Exponents · Lesson 2 of 3

A negative exponent sends a factor across the fraction bar

x−3 = 1x3. The minus sign on an exponent doesn't make anything negative. It tells you the factor belongs on the other side of the fraction bar.

All four rules from Lesson 1 still work with negative exponents. This lesson adds one move, and shows where it goes wrong. No calculator needed.

2 · The move

Move it across the bar and the sign flips

The move

A factor with a negative exponent is on the wrong side of the fraction bar. Move it across, and its exponent turns positive. Factors with positive exponents stay where they are.

Example a−nckb−m = bmckan

Only factors move

In a−1 + bc, the a−1 is added to b. It's a term, not a factor, so it can't cross the bar on its own.

3 · The sign

A negative exponent doesn't make the value negative

2−2 is not −4, and not −¼. It's ¼. To see why, walk down the powers of 2. Each step down divides by 2, and that keeps going past zero.

4 · Negative bases

When the base itself is negative

A negative value only comes from a negative base. Watch the brackets: they decide what the base is.

Evaluate(−2)−3
  1. 1(−2)3Move the factor across. The base is −2, brackets and all.
  2. 1−8(−2)(−2)(−2) = −8. An odd number of negatives multiplies to a negative.
  3. −18The answer is negative because the base was negative, not because of the exponent.
Brackets: base is −2
(−2)−2 = 1(−2)2 = 14 Two negatives multiply to a positive.
No brackets: base is 2
−2−2 = −122 = −14 The exponent only touches the 2. The minus sign waits outside.

Positive or negative?

Don't work out the number. Just decide its sign.

5 · Lesson 1's rules, with negatives

Negatives inside brackets

The Lesson 1 rules work exactly the same with negative exponents. Just be careful with the signs: x−3 · x5 = x−3+5 = x2.

The one that costs marks is a power of a power. It still just multiplies the exponents, signs included. Multiply first. Only then check whether the result is negative.

Negative times negative
(x−5)−2 = x10 (−5)(−2) = +10. Positive, so nothing moves.
Negative times positive
(x−5)2 = x−10 = 1x10 (−5)(2) = −10. Negative, so now it moves.
Simplify2−2(x−5)−2
  1. 2−2x10Power of a power: (−5)(−2) = 10.
  2. x1022Only 2−2 is on the wrong side. Move it down. The x10 stays on top.
  3. x10422 = 4. No negative exponents left, so it's finished.
