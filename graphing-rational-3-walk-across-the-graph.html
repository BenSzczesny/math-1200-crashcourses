1 · The method

Start at the right end and walk left

What you bring
  • The ends: leading term over leading term, then simplify. Plus is above on the right, minus is below. Even or just a number: the left end is on the same side. Odd: the other side.
  • The key numbers: at each zero or vertical asymptote, an odd power changes the sign and an even power keeps it. The sign just means which side of the x-axis the graph is on: above is positive, below is negative.
  1. Factor and cancel. Mark the key numbers.
  2. Right end: above or below, from the leading terms.
  3. Walk left. At each key number, switch sides if the power is odd. Stay on the same side if the power is even.
  4. Check: you must arrive at the left end the leading terms gave you. If you don't, a power was misread.

The check works because the ends and the powers come from the same factors. So they have to agree.

2 · Fractions

Asymptotes join the walk

An asymptote is one more key number. Knowing the side tells you which way the graph goes beside it:

Beside a vertical asymptote A graph that's above the axis shoots up. A graph that's below the axis shoots down.

  1. zero −2 (odd), asymptote x = 3 (odd)Top and bottom, each with power 1.

3 · More key numbers

Two asymptotes, and holes

More key numbers only means more steps. Take them one at a time.

Sketchf(x) = x(x − 2)(x + 2)
  1. zero 0, asymptotes x = 2 and x = −2All odd, so the sign changes at all three.
  2. ends: xx2 = 1xy = 0. Just above on the right, just below on the left.
  3. start above; change at 2Right of 2 it's above, so it shoots up there. Odd, so between 0 and 2 it's below and shoots down.
  4. change at 0Odd at 0, so it crosses to above.
  5. change at −2Between −2 and 0 it's above, so it shoots up there. Odd, so left of −2 it's below and shoots down.
  6. arrives below on the left ✓Three changes turned above into below, and the left end agrees.

Holes

A hole changes nothing in the walk. Cancel the factor, sketch what's left, then put an open circle on the curve at the hole. Its height comes from putting its x into what's left, and that also tells you which side of the axis it's on.
