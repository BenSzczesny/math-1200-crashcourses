Clear the fractions with one multiplication

Multiply both sides of an equation by the right thing, and all the fractions disappear. Choose that thing well, multiply every term by it, and check your answer at the end.

1 · The idea

One multiplication and the fractions are gone

Multiply both sides by a number that every denominator divides into. Each denominator cancels.

Solvex2 + x3 = 5
  1. 6 · x2 + 6 · x3 = 6 · 5Multiply every term by 6. Both 2 and 3 divide into 6.
  2. 3x + 2x = 306 ÷ 2 = 3 and 6 ÷ 3 = 2. No fractions left.
  3. x = 65x = 30. Check: 6/2 + 6/3 = 3 + 2 = 5.
What to multiply by the smallest thing every denominator divides into: the LCD
2 · Choosing it

Build the LCD from factors

Multiplying all the denominators together always works, but it usually gives you something too big. Then you have to cancel the extra back out at the end.

Build it
denominators 6x2 and 2x LCD = 6x2 2x already divides into 6x2. Nothing extra needed.
Multiplying them together
6x2 · 2x = 12x3 You'd get the same answer, but every term carries an extra 2x.
  1. Factor every denominator.Numbers into primes, expressions into brackets.
  2. List each different factor once.
  3. Use the highest power each one reaches.If one denominator has x and another has x2, use x2.
  4. Multiply those together. That's the LCD.

3 · The other half

Every term gets multiplied, even the plain numbers

A term with no fraction still gets multiplied. It just doesn't cancel anything.

Solve1x + 2 = 3x
  1. x · 1x + x · 2 = x · 3xThe LCD is x. Multiply all three terms.
  2. 1 + 2x = 3The fractions cancel. The middle term becomes 2x: nothing to cancel, so the x stays.
  3. x = 12x = 2. And 1 ≠ 0, so it's allowed (see section 4).
Watch out

If you skip the 2, you get 1 + 2 = 3. That's true for every x, so it tells you nothing, and it looks like a sensible line.

4 · The last step

Some answers have to be thrown out

If the LCD contains x, it can be zero for some values of x. Multiplying both sides by zero turns any equation into 0 = 0, so the new equation can have answers that the original doesn't.

Do this every time

Before you start: write down every value of x that makes a denominator zero. Those are banned.

After you solve: if your answer is on the banned list, throw it out.

Solvexx − 3 = 3x − 3 + 2
  1. banned: x = 3It makes x − 3 zero. Write this down first.
  2. x = 3 + 2(x − 3)Multiply every term by the LCD, x − 3. The 2 becomes 2(x − 3).
  3. x = 2x − 3Expand: 3 + 2x − 6 = 2x − 3.
  4. x = 3Subtract 2x: −x = −3.
  5. no solution3 is banned. The algebra was right, but this answer doesn't work in the original. It's called extraneous.

Cheat sheet

Solving an equation with fractions

  1. Write down the banned values.Anything that makes a denominator zero.
  2. Factor the denominators and build the LCD.Each factor once, at its highest power.
  3. Multiply every term on both sides by the LCD.Plain numbers too.
  4. Expand and solve.
