1 · The idea

Two machines in a row

Take f(x) = x2 and g(x) = x + 3. Feed a number into one, and its output into the other. Then swap the order.

Composition (f ∘ g)(x) = f(g(x)) read right to left: g is next to x, so g goes first
Not multiplying

f ∘ g passes the output along. f · g multiplies the two outputs. Different things.

2 · With a number

Inside first

f(x) = 2x − 1,  g(x) = x2 + 1. Find(f ∘ g)(2)
  1. g(2) = 22 + 1 = 5g is on the inside, so it goes first.
  2. f(5) = 2(5) − 1 = 9Hand the 5 to f.

The other order, (g ∘ f)(2), is f(2) = 3, then g(3) = 10. The order changes the answer.

5 · Advanced

A fraction inside, and the domain

f(x) = 3x + 2,  g(x) = 2x − 1. Find(g ∘ f)(x)
  1. g(f(x)) = 2 · 3x + 2 − 1All of f(x) replaces the x in 2x − 1.
  2. = 6x + 2 − x + 2x + 2Write the 1 over the same denominator.
  3. = 6 − x − 2x + 2 = 4 − xx + 2The minus reaches both terms of x + 2.
  4. domain: x ≠ −2f goes first, and f can't take −2.
The domain of a composition

x has to work in g, and g(x) has to work in f. Check the original functions, not just the simplified answer.

f(x) = 1x,  g(x) = x − 5. Find (f ∘ g)(x) and its domain
6 · In words

Word problems: which happens first?

The function that acts first goes on the inside.

An oil spill's radius is r(t) = 4t meters after t hours. Its area is A(r) = πr2. Find the area after t hours, then after 2 hours.
  1. A(r(t)) = π(4t)2Time gives the radius, and the radius gives the area. So r goes inside.
  2. = 16πt2(4t)2 = 16t2: square the 4 too.
  3. 16π(2)2 = 64π square metersPut t = 2 into the composed function. Leave π as π.
