Write the power out, and the rules explain themselves

1 · What it means

The exponent is a count

x5 is not "x times 5". It is five x's multiplied together. Pick a base and an exponent:

Base Exponent
=
Two things to know
  • x1 = x. A letter on its own counts as one factor.
  • An exponent only applies to what it touches. In 2x3 only the x is cubed: 2 · x · x · x.

4 · Rule 3

A power of a power: multiply the exponents

(x3)2 means two copies of x3 multiplied together. Write both copies and count.

(x3)2 = x3 · x3 =
2 groups of 3 = 6 factors of x = x6
Power of a power (am)n = amn

This one gets mixed up with Rule 1. The brackets decide it.

No brackets: add
x3 · x2 = x5 Two powers side by side. Three x's and two x's make five.
Brackets: multiply
(x3)2 = x6 One power, copied twice. Two groups of three make six.
