Factoring · Part 3 of 3

Two numbers do all the work

Factoring a quadratic comes down to one question: which two numbers multiply to one thing and add to another? The diamond is where you write it down.

1 · Where it comes from

Multiply two brackets and look at the result

Multiply out(x + p)(x + q)
  1. x2 + qx + px + pqEach term in the first bracket times each term in the second.
  2. x2 + (p + q)x + pqThe middle number is p + q. The last number is pq.

So factoring x2 + 7x + 12 means finding two numbers that multiply to 12 and add to 7.

The question, every time multiply to ___    add to ___
2 · The tool

The diamond

Top: the product. Bottom: the sum. Sides: the two numbers you're looking for. It puts both conditions in front of you, so a pair that only passes one of them looks wrong.

For x2 + 7x + 12:

  1. Top: 12. Bottom: 7.
  2. Pairs that multiply to 12: 1 and 12, 2 and 6, 3 and 4.
  3. Only 3 and 4 add to 7.

3 · Save time

Work out the signs first

The signs of the two numbers are decided by the top and bottom of the diamond. Work them out before you search.

Product +, sum +
Both positive
x2 + 7x + 12: 3 and 4
Product +, sum −
Both negative
x2 − 7x + 12: −3 and −4
Product −
One of each
The bigger one gets the sign of the sum. x2 + x − 12: 4 and −3

Drill: pick the pair

Before searching: what are the two signs in?
4 · Easy case

When the front number is 1, the pair goes straight in

Factorx2 + 3x − 18
  1. multiply to −18, add to 3Product negative: one of each sign. Sum positive: the bigger one is positive.
  2. 6 and −3Pairs for 18: 1 & 18, 2 & 9, 3 & 6. Only 6 and −3 add to 3.
  3. (x + 6)(x − 3)Put them in the brackets. Check the middle: −3x + 6x = 3x.
Only when the front number is 1

For 2x2, 3x2 and so on, the pair does not go straight in. See section 5.

5 · Harder case

When the front number isn't 1

For ax2 + bx + c, two things change:

Two changes
  • The top of the diamond is a · c, not c.
  • The two numbers split the middle term. Then factor by grouping (Part 2).

10y2 + 27y + 5

  1. Top: 10 · 5 = 50. Bottom: 27.
  2. Pairs for 50: 1 & 50, 2 & 25, 5 & 10.
  3. Only 2 and 25 add to 27.
Factor10y2 + 27y + 5
  1. 10y2 + 25y + 2y + 5Split 27y into 25y + 2y.
  2. 5y(2y + 5) + 1(2y + 5)Group in pairs. The same bracket twice means the split was right.
  3. (2y + 5)(5y + 1)Pull out the shared bracket. (Splitting as 2y + 25y gives the same answer.)

6 · Always first

Take out the common factor first

A common factor makes the diamond's top number bigger. Take it out first and the search gets easier.

Left in
6x2 + 15x + 6 Top is 6 · 6 = 36. Lots of pairs to check.
Taken out first
3(2x2 + 5x + 2) Top is 2 · 2 = 4. The pair 4 and 1 is quick. Answer: 3(2x + 1)(x + 2).

The 3 stays in the answer. It was moved to the front, not cancelled.

7 · Pattern

Perfect square trinomials

Sometimes the two numbers are the same, and the answer is a bracket squared.

The shapes a2 + 2ab + b2 = (a + b)2 a2 − 2ab + b2 = (a − b)2
Factor4x2 + 12x + 9
  1. (2x)2 and 32Are the first and last terms squares? 4x2 = (2x)2 and 9 = 32. Yes.
  2. 2 · 2x · 3 = 12xIs the middle term twice their product? Yes.
  3. (2x + 3)2The middle sign is +, so it's (a + b)2. (The diamond gives the same: top 36, bottom 12, pair 6 and 6.)

8 · Your turn

Try any quadratic

Set a, b and c. The page takes out any common factor, fills in the diamond, lists every pair with the right signs, and finds the one that works. Try your homework problems here after doing them on paper.

When no pair works

If you've listed every pair and none adds to the bottom number, the quadratic doesn't factor with integers. That's a real answer. Try x2 + 4x + 6 above: 1 and 6 add to 7, 2 and 3 add to 5, and that's every pair.

9 · Common mistakes

Four wrong factorings

Put x = 1 into both sides. If they disagree, the factoring is wrong.

Cheat sheet

Factoring ax2 + bx + c

  1. Take out any common factor. It stays in the answer.
  2. Diamond: top a · c, bottom b.
  3. Work out the signs, then list pairs.Product +: same signs (the sum's sign). Product −: one of each.
  4. a = 1: the pair goes straight into the brackets.a ≠ 1: split the middle term and group.
  5. Multiply back to check. No pair works: doesn't factor with integers.
